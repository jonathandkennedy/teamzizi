# ==== cited_source_patterns ====

## What Earns Citations in This Market

### The source mix, ranked by how often it fed AI answers

1. **Agent-owned blogs/sites (the dominant citable class).** For every non-directory query type — pros/cons, should-I-buy, market updates, even "best agent" — the AI answers were substantially written from individual agents' and teams' own content: carmelvalley.com (Sez Sezer), sandiegorealestatehunter.com, felicialewisgroup.com, findyourhomesandiego.com (Scott Cheng), luxurycoastgroup.com (Barry Estates), sandiegoexperts.com (Butler Group), totalsocalhomes.com, dawnsellssandiego.com, luxurysocalrealty.com, brizolisjanzen.com, sdhousingmarket.com. The formats that win: exact-match H1/title ("Top Real Estate Agents in [Neighborhood]", "Pros and Cons of Living in [Neighborhood]"), **year-stamped titles ("2026", "2026-2027")**, FAQ blocks, ~2,000–2,500 words, concrete local specifics (school names, DOM, median price), refreshed monthly market-report series. The exemplar page I inspected (carmelvalley.com pros/cons) is exactly this template.

2. **Agent directories/aggregators (own the "best realtor" SERP shell).** Yelp, Zillow agent-reviews, FastExpert, HomeLight, U.S. News realestate, Homes.com agents, Agent Pronto, Redfin, Coldwell Banker city pages. These rank 1–3 on nearly every "best realtor [place]" query, so being listed and review-rich inside them is table stakes for being named in AI answers.

3. **Portals for numbers.** Zillow home-values, Redfin market pages, Movoto trends, Houzeo, Trulia supply the statistics in every market query — but AI answers consistently blend a portal's numbers with a local agent blog's interpretation. The interpretation slot is the winnable one.

4. **Homes.com neighborhood guides + moving-company blogs (republicmoving.com, PODS)** — recurring on lifestyle queries.

5. **Press releases work here.** Two syndicated press releases (Newswire/AccessNewswire) took the top 2 organic spots AND the AI answer for "best real estate agent Scripps Ranch." The citation bar in this market is low enough that placed content wins head terms.

6. **What's largely ABSENT: Reddit, local news, and Niche.** Niche appeared once (Scripps Ranch). Reddit never surfaced in any tested SERP; the UGC that does rank is decades-old City-Data forum threads (4S Ranch). No union-tribune/local-news results on any query. This market's citation graph is agent-blog-and-directory shaped, not community-content shaped — very different from big-metro patterns.

### Implications for Team Azizi

- **The moat is shallow.** Solo agents with basic Wordpress blogs (Dawn, Melina Rissone, Wesley Guest) are getting cited by AI for market queries. A 15-agent, $90M team publishing the same formats with better data should displace them quickly.
- **Directory presence is the prerequisite for "best realtor" answers.** Team Azizi is in none of the aggregators that frame those answers (FastExpert, HomeLight, U.S. News, Yelp-as-team). Reviews live scattered across Zillow/experience.com under individual names.
- **Their own brand answer is being written by third-party directories** (wrong old address, "45 Ranch" typo) because teamazizi.com is down. Every day the site is down, cityof.com and housing.info remain the authoritative sources about them.
- **Compass affiliation is not enough** — other Compass teams win intra-Compass queries via their own profile copy and owned sites.
