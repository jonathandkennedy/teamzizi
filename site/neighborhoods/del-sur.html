Del Sur Real Estate Guide

92127 · Poway Unified School District

Home › Neighborhoods › Del Sur

Written by Zohra Azizi, CA DRE# 01992847 · Updated 2026-07-25

What follows is the part of a Del Sur search that is hard to look up: which tax districts apply, which district assigns the schools, and what we have actually done here. Every figure names its source.

Does Del Sur have Mello-Roos, and how much?

Del Sur does carry Mello-Roos. The County Auditor's active FY 2025-26 list shows 2 community facilities districts covering Del Sur, and a home there can sit inside more than one.

  • Poway Unified CFD #12 (Blk Mtn Rch) — district 6122-20, administered by KeyAnalytics, (877) 575-0265
  • Black Mountain Ranch Villages CFD #4 — district 6086-09, administered by Charmane Custodio, (888) 892-2480

Del Sur sits inside the Black Mountain Ranch master plan and inside Poway Unified. Homes there can carry both a school-district CFD and a community CFD — which is why two similar homes on the same street can have materially different tax bills.

A Mello-Roos amount is specific to the parcel, not the neighborhood: it varies by district, improvement area and the phase a home was built in. The authoritative figure is the line item on the property tax bill, which names the district and gives a contact number.

Source: County of San Diego Auditor & Controller, Active Mello-Roos Districts (CFD) for FY 2025-2026, retrieved 2026-07-25.

What school district serves Del Sur?

Del Sur is served by Poway Unified School District. Attendance is assigned by address rather than by ZIP code, so two homes a few streets apart can feed different schools — confirm the specific address with the district before relying on it.

When does Mello-Roos end in Del Sur?

A Del Sur Mello-Roos levy ends when the bonds it services are retired, and the remaining term is set in the formation documents for the specific district rather than being a single Del Sur-wide date. Because Del Sur homes can sit inside both Poway Unified CFD #12 and Black Mountain Ranch Villages CFD #4, a parcel can have two terms running on two different schedules.

One distinction is worth understanding before assuming an end date. Many community facilities districts levy for two things: a bond component that retires when the debt is paid, and a services component that funds ongoing maintenance and does not expire. A district can therefore “end” and still leave an annual charge on the bill.

The district administrators named on the tax bill will state the remaining term for a specific parcel. That is the only answer worth acting on.

Del Sur or 4S Ranch — what actually differs?

Del Sur and 4S Ranch share a ZIP code, a school district and a Mello-Roos burden, and differ mainly in age and layout. Del Sur is the newer of the two, built out through the 2000s and 2010s inside the Black Mountain Ranch master plan around an unusually large program of pools, parks, gardens and trails. 4S Ranch is slightly older and organised around a larger retail core.

The tax detail differs too, and it is not a matter of one being cheaper. Del Sur parcels can carry Poway Unified CFD #12 alongside Black Mountain Ranch Villages CFD #4; 4S Ranch parcels sit under Poway Unified CFD #6 or #10 and their improvement areas. Which specific districts apply, and at what amount, is a parcel-level fact on the tax bill rather than a community characteristic.

Buyers weighing either against a lower tax bill in the same school district should also look at the city of Poway, which is inside Poway Unified but largely predates these districts.

Is Del Sur hotter than the coast?

Del Sur sits inland of the coastal ridgelines, and summer afternoons there run warmer than Del Mar or Carmel Valley while the marine layer clears earlier in the day. That is the standard inland-versus-coastal pattern in San Diego County rather than anything specific to the community, and the National Weather Service San Diego office publishes the actual climate normals for anyone who wants the figures instead of the impression.

The practical consequence is cooling load. An inland home of the same size and vintage will generally run its air conditioning more than a coastal one, which is worth asking a seller about directly — utility history is disclosable and far more informative than a temperature average.

What amenities does Del Sur have?

Del Sur was planned around a shared amenity program rather than a single clubhouse: multiple neighborhood pools, a trail network connecting the villages, parks and community gardens, all administered by the Del Sur Community Association. Access comes with the property rather than by separate membership.

That program is funded by the HOA assessment, which sits alongside the Mello-Roos levy rather than replacing it. A true cost comparison against a community with fewer shared facilities has to count both, and the association’s current budget and reserve study — both disclosable documents — are where the real numbers are.
