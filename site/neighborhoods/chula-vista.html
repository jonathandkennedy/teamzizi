What follows is the part of a Chula Vista search that is hard to look up: which tax districts apply, which district assigns the schools, and what we have actually done here. Every figure names its source.

Does Chula Vista have Mello-Roos, and how much?

Chula Vista carries the densest Mello-Roos concentration in the county list — 33 city district lines across the east-side master plans (Otay Ranch, Eastlake, Millenia, Sunbow), plus separate districts levied by the elementary and high school districts — so a single east-side parcel can carry city, elementary and high-school special-tax lines at once.

  • City districts — Otay Ranch villages, Eastlake, Millenia, Sunbow; 33 lines in the county list — district 6014-xx / 6015-xx, administered by Spicer Consulting Group, (866) 504-2067
  • Chula Vista Elementary CFDs #1–#21 — district 3798-xx, administered by Chula Vista Elementary School District, (619) 425-9600
  • Sweetwater Union High CFDs #1–#6 — district 6159-xx, administered by Sweetwater Union High School District, (619) 691-5575

The split is geographic: western Chula Vista's older stock largely predates the Mello-Roos era, while nearly all of the load sits east of Interstate 805. Which districts apply, and at what amount, is parcel-specific — the tax bill names every line.

A Mello-Roos amount is specific to the parcel, not the neighborhood: it varies by district, improvement area and the phase a home was built in. The authoritative figure is the line item on the property tax bill, which names the district and gives a contact number.

Source: County of San Diego Auditor & Controller, Active Mello-Roos Districts (CFD) for FY 2025-2026, retrieved 2026-07-25.

What school district serves Chula Vista?

Chula Vista is served by Chula Vista Elementary School District (K-6) and Sweetwater Union High School District. Attendance is assigned by address rather than by ZIP code, so two homes a few streets apart can feed different schools — confirm the specific address with the district before relying on it.

Why do eastern and western Chula Vista feel like different markets?

Chula Vista splits at Interstate 805: western Chula Vista is the older city — mid-century stock, largely free of Mello-Roos — while the east side is master-planned (Eastlake, Otay Ranch, Millenia) and carries the densest community-facilities-district concentration in the county list.

The same list price therefore buys two different monthly payments, and the Mello-Roos block above is where the difference lives — a single east-side parcel can carry city, elementary-district and high-school-district special-tax lines at once. Comparing east to west without the tax lines is comparing nothing.

Why does Chula Vista have two school districts instead of one?

Chula Vista has no unified school district: Chula Vista Elementary School District — among the largest elementary-only districts in California — runs kindergarten through sixth grade, and Sweetwater Union High School District runs seventh through twelfth.

Both districts also levy their own Mello-Roos on the east side, which is the unusual part — the school-assignment question and the school-tax question are separate checks on the same address. Assignment itself follows each district’s boundary maps, confirmed with the district for the exact address.

What is happening on the Chula Vista bayfront?

The Chula Vista Bayfront is mid-transformation: the Gaylord Pacific resort and convention center opened in 2025 as the anchor of a decades-planned redevelopment of the industrial waterfront, with further phases entitled around it.

Waterfront redevelopment moves on public documents — the port district’s master plan and its phase approvals — and its effects on the west side are the trackable kind: employment, traffic patterns, and what happens to the blocks between the bay and Broadway. Watching the filings beats watching the renderings.

Check the record: official Chula Vista sources

Every figure above names its source. These are the official pages where those records actually live — verified August 2026, and worth more than any summary of them, ours included.

  • City of Chula Vista Development Services — permits, zoning and code questions for either side of the 805.
  • Chula Vista Elementary School District — the K-6 district — enrollment and school assignment.
  • CVESD community facilities districts portal — the elementary district's own CFD lookup — parcel number in, current tax and final year out.
  • Sweetwater Union High CFD records — the 7-12 district's Mello-Roos reports and formation documents, on its own fiscal-services site.
  • Chula Vista short-term rental rules — the city's own STR ordinance — separate from San Diego's system entirely.
  • Chula Vista Bayfront project — the city's page for the 535-acre waterfront build-out.
  • County special-assessments lookup — enter a parcel number for the current-year breakdown of every fixed charge on the tax bill.
