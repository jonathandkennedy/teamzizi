What follows is the part of a Carmel Valley search that is hard to look up: which tax districts apply, which district assigns the schools, and what we have actually done here. Every figure names its source.

Does Carmel Valley have Mello-Roos, and how much?

No community facilities district in the County Auditor's active FY 2025-26 list is named for Carmel Valley. That is the documented basis for saying Carmel Valley homes generally do not carry Mello-Roos — though it describes district names, not a guarantee for any particular parcel.

No community facilities district in the County Auditor's active FY 2025-26 list is named for Carmel Valley or Torrey Highlands. Neighbouring Black Mountain Ranch villages do carry districts, so the boundary matters and the tax bill is the place to confirm it.

A Mello-Roos amount is specific to the parcel, not the neighborhood: it varies by district, improvement area and the phase a home was built in. The authoritative figure is the line item on the property tax bill, which names the district and gives a contact number.

Source: County of San Diego Auditor & Controller, Active Mello-Roos Districts (CFD) for FY 2025-2026, retrieved 2026-07-25.

What school district serves Carmel Valley?

Carmel Valley is served by Del Mar Union / San Dieguito Union (boundary-dependent). Attendance is assigned by address rather than by ZIP code, so two homes a few streets apart can feed different schools — confirm the specific address with the district before relying on it.
