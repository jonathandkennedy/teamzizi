Fallbrook Real Estate Guide

What follows is the part of a Fallbrook search that is hard to look up: which tax districts apply, which district assigns the schools, and what we have actually done here. Every figure names its source.

Does Fallbrook have Mello-Roos, and how much?

No community facilities district in the County Auditor's active FY 2025-26 list is named for Fallbrook. That is the documented basis for saying Fallbrook homes generally do not carry Mello-Roos — though it describes district names, not a guarantee for any particular parcel.

No community facilities district in the active list is named for Fallbrook. Rural parcels there raise different cost questions instead — well, septic and fire insurance.

A Mello-Roos amount is specific to the parcel, not the neighborhood: it varies by district, improvement area and the phase a home was built in. The authoritative figure is the line item on the property tax bill, which names the district and gives a contact number.

Source: County of San Diego Auditor & Controller, Active Mello-Roos Districts (CFD) for FY 2025-2026, retrieved 2026-07-25.

What school district serves Fallbrook?

Fallbrook is served by Fallbrook Union Elementary and Fallbrook Union High School District. Attendance is assigned by address rather than by ZIP code, so two homes a few streets apart can feed different schools — confirm the specific address with the district before relying on it.

Is Fallbrook a city?

Fallbrook is not an incorporated city. It is an unincorporated community governed directly by the County of San Diego, which means there is no Fallbrook city council, no city building department and no city zoning code. Land-use questions there go to the County of San Diego Planning & Development Services rather than to a city planning counter, and there is no city council to appeal to — the Board of Supervisors sits at the top of that process instead.

The Fallbrook Community Planning Group, a county-recognised advisory body, reviews projects and makes recommendations, but the decisions are the County's. For a buyer planning an addition, a second unit or a lot split, that changes both the timeline and the counter to approach — and it is the first thing to establish, not the last.

Do Fallbrook homes have well water and septic?

Many Fallbrook properties, particularly the larger parcels, are on private wells and septic systems rather than on municipal water and sewer — while homes closer to the village core are more often served by Fallbrook Public Utility District. Which applies is a parcel-level question, and the answer changes the inspection list, the financing and the running cost.

A well means testing yield and water quality, not just confirming a well exists. A septic system means a functional inspection and locating the leach field, because replacing one is a five-figure item. Neither shows up in an automated valuation, and neither is optional to check.

Can you get fire insurance in Fallbrook?

Fire insurance availability is the question to settle first on a Fallbrook property, before the inspection and ideally before the offer. Much of the community sits in a state-designated high or very high fire hazard severity zone, and carriers have narrowed what they will write in those zones — which can leave the California FAIR Plan plus a difference-in-conditions policy as the route to coverage.

The cost gap between a standard policy and that combination is large enough to change what a buyer can afford, and a lender will not fund without bound coverage. Getting a quote on the specific address during the contingency period, rather than assuming a rate, is the difference between a smooth close and a collapse at the end of escrow.

What should I know about Fallbrook's avocado groves?

Fallbrook has a long agricultural history built on avocado and citrus, and a working grove attached to a home is a business with costs rather than a garden. Irrigation water is the dominant one, and agricultural water rates, grove maintenance and harvest logistics all need pricing before a buyer treats acreage as a straightforward amenity.

There is also an assessment question worth raising early. Land under a Williamson Act contract carries a reduced property tax assessment in exchange for a commitment to keep it in agricultural use, and those contracts run with the land and take years to unwind. Whether a parcel is under one is a matter of public record and should be established before the plans for it are.

Check the record: official Fallbrook sources

Every figure above names its source. These are the official pages where those records actually live — verified August 2026, and worth more than any summary of them, ours included.

  • County of San Diego Planning & Development Services — the land-use and permitting authority for unincorporated communities — there is no city planning counter here.
  • Fallbrook Union Elementary School District — the K-8 district.
  • Fallbrook Union High School District — the separate high-school district.
  • Fallbrook Public Utility District — water and wastewater provider.
  • North County Fire Protection District — fire and EMS for Fallbrook, Bonsall and Rainbow — the official domain is now .gov.
  • State Fire Marshal — Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps — the address-lookup viewer for a parcel's official fire hazard severity zone designation.
