What follows is the part of a Menifee search that is hard to look up: which tax districts apply, which district assigns the schools, and what we have actually done here. Every figure names its source.

Does Menifee have Mello-Roos, and how much?

Menifee does carry Mello-Roos, and more of it on newer homes: the City of Menifee established four original bond districts and, since April 2015, a citywide maintenance district that every new development annexes into — thirty-four development zones so far — while the school and water districts levy separately.

  • Citywide maintenance CFD — every new development annexes in; 34 zones to date — district 2015-2, administered by City of Menifee, menifee.ca.gov
  • Original city bond districts — district four formed at incorporation era, administered by City of Menifee, menifee.ca.gov
  • Menifee Union School District CFDs — district 2014-3 among them, administered by the school district, menifeeusd.org

Menifee's special taxes are billed through the County of Riverside and appear as separate line items on the property tax bill — the same reading skill the San Diego pages teach applies here, with different district names. Eastern Municipal Water District participates in community facilities financing for new development as well.

A Mello-Roos amount is specific to the parcel, not the neighborhood: it varies by district, improvement area and the phase a home was built in. The authoritative figure is the line item on the property tax bill, which names the district and gives a contact number.

Source: City of Menifee, Special Districts (LLMD, CSA & CFD), retrieved 2026-07-30.

What school district serves Menifee?

Menifee is served by Menifee Union School District (K-8) and Perris Union High School District. Attendance is assigned by address rather than by ZIP code, so two homes a few streets apart can feed different schools — confirm the specific address with the district before relying on it.

When did Menifee become a city?

Menifee incorporated in 2008, making it one of Riverside County’s newest cities — a municipal government younger than much of its housing stock, which is why districts formed under the county still appear on Menifee tax bills alongside the city’s own.

Incorporation date is not trivia here: it explains the layered special-district landscape the Mello-Roos block documents, and it explains why service questions — roads, lighting, landscape maintenance — route to different agencies depending on when a tract was built.

Why does Menifee span three school systems?

Menifee splits across school systems the way Escondido does: Menifee Union School District runs kindergarten through eighth grade for most of the city, Perris Union High School District runs the high schools, and the northern Romoland area is served by Romoland School District for the elementary years.

Elementary and high-school assignment are therefore two separate questions with two separate answers, and a north-Menifee address needs the third check. The verification method is the same one that governs every guide on this site: the district office and the exact street address, never the city name.

What is Sun City, and is all of Menifee age-restricted?

Sun City is the historic core of Menifee — a Del Webb retirement development dating to the early 1960s — and its age-qualified communities operate under federal senior-housing rules, while most of modern Menifee is conventional all-ages housing.

Age qualification is a recorded, community-specific legal status — not something to assume from the Sun City name in either direction. The governing documents state it, escrow discloses it, and a buyer or heir dealing with a specific property should verify that community’s status rather than the neighborhood’s reputation.

Who provides water and sewer in Menifee?

Water and sewer service in Menifee comes from Eastern Municipal Water District rather than city hall, and EMWD participates in community facilities financing for new development — one more line on a new-construction tax bill that predates any individual buyer.

On the corridor’s newer tracts the practical consequence is a tax bill with city, school and water lines from three different agencies — each with its own contact, each answerable, none of them guessable from the listing. The Mello-Roos block above names the districts and sources.

Check the record: official Menifee sources

Every figure above names its source. These are the official pages where those records actually live — verified August 2026, and worth more than any summary of them, ours included.

  • City of Menifee — city hall — the official domain is menifee.ca.gov; the older .us address redirects here.
  • Menifee Union School District — the TK-8 district for most of the city.
  • Perris Union High School District — the secondary district for much of Menifee.
  • Romoland School District — the TK-8 district for the city's northeast — Menifee spans three school systems.
  • Eastern Municipal Water District — water and sewer provider.
