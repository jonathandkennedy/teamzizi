How many homes has Team Azizi sold?

Team Azizi, based in San Diego, has 1,016 closed sales and 43 closed rentals recorded on its Compass profile, with 2025 production of $105.59M across 92 transaction sides.

That 2025 figure is third-party verifiable: it ranks the team #58 in California by volume on the 2026 Best Real Estate Large Teams in California from RealTrends Verified, reporting 2025 production. The San Diego Business Journal named the team one of the top 10 in the county in October 2025.

Where does Team Azizi actually sell?

Team Azizi’s largest markets by transaction count are in inland and coastal North County — Escondido above all, then Oceanside, Fallbrook and San Marcos — alongside the I-15 corridor communities of Del Sur, 4S Ranch and Scripps Ranch and the coast at Carmel Valley and Del Mar.
