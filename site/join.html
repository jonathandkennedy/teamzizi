Real estate agents

What kind of agent fits an area-farming team?

Team Azizi covers 31 San Diego County and Temecula Valley communities with 19 licensees, and each neighborhood guide names the licensee who covers that area — so the agent this model suits is one who wants to own a place rather than work a queue of leads from anywhere.

Depth is the whole method. Every neighborhood guide carries that community’s Mello-Roos position traced to the County Auditor’s active district list, which district assigns the schools and where the boundaries actually run, and what the team has sold there. An agent joining inherits that surface for their area — and inherits the obligation to keep it accurate, because a guide that rots is worse than no guide.

What is Team Azizi's track record?

Team Azizi closed $105.59M across 92 transaction sides in 2025 in San Diego County, against 1,016 closed sales in the team’s Compass record, and ranks #58 of all California large teams by volume on RealTrends Verified’s 2026 list.

The San Diego Business Journal named the team one of the top 10 real estate teams in the county in October 2025. Both placements are third-party published rather than self-reported, which is the point: the RealTrends ranking can be opened and read before you talk to anyone here. No figure on this site requires taking the team’s word for it, and that standard applies to recruiting as much as to the sold record.

What marketing is already running that I would join?

The marketing surface a licensee joins at Team Azizi is already built and public across San Diego County: 31 neighborhood guides, a journal of sourced answer posts, and an official-resources layer linking the districts, county offices and agencies each community answers to.

What that is worth to an individual agent is specific. The Mello-Roos lookup answers the most-repeated buyer question in this county, which no competitor page here answers at all; the journal answers the tax, insurance, school-boundary and development questions that decide transactions. Pages built this way get retrieved and cited by AI assistants as well as ranked, and the licensee named on the page is the one the citation points to.

Which brokerage would I hang my licence with?

Licensees on Team Azizi are affiliated with Compass — Compass California III, Inc., CA DRE# 01527365 — working from the Compass office at 12860 El Camino Real, Suite 100 in Carmel Valley, San Diego.

Team Azizi is a team within that brokerage rather than an independent brokerage, so the licence, the transaction file and the errors-and-omissions cover sit with Compass, and Compass programmes are available to the team’s clients — Concierge being the one that most often decides whether a listing goes to market ready. Terms of any brokerage programme are set by Compass and change; ask for the current ones in writing.

What about splits, caps, fees and lead flow?

Splits, caps, desk fees and lead flow are not published on this page, and any San Diego County team that publishes them on a recruiting page is describing an average rather than the deal you would actually sign.

Those terms depend on the role, the licensee’s experience and the territory, so they are a conversation rather than a web page — and the honest advice to any agent evaluating any team, this one included, is the same: get the split, the cap, every recurring fee, who owns the leads and who owns the client relationship in writing before a licence moves. A team unwilling to put those in writing has told you the answer.

How do you apply to join Team Azizi?

Applications to Team Azizi go through the form below — name, contact details, a California DRE licence number if you hold one, and which San Diego County or Temecula Valley communities you know best.

Licence status is the first thing worth stating plainly: California requires a salesperson to be licensed and affiliated with a broker, and every team member is named on this site with their DRE number for that reason. If you are still in the exam process, say so — it is a timing question, not a disqualification. The form reaches the team directly, and a first conversation commits you to nothing.

Start with a question, not a commitment

Send the form and a member of the team replies directly. If you are weighing two or three teams in San Diego County at once, ask all of them the questions in the terms section above — the answers will separate them faster than any careers page can.
